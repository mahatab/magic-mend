## Decision (reviewed)

ChatGPT runs through OpenAI's official `codex app-server` (version 0.163, from the Codex desktop app) on a ChatGPT login, with the rewrite prompt as the only instructions, an ephemeral read-only thread, low reasoning effort, and tools, plugins, skills, memories, hooks, project docs, and the user's MCP servers switched off. Priming is a Claude feature, so only cold and warm runs were measured.

**Model: GPT-6-Luna (default), the others available in Settings.**

- Every model passed the no-contractions and no-em-dash check in 20 of 20 warm runs without a fix round, and all three corrected "i has send the payment" to "I sent the payment".
- GPT-5.6-Luna is the fastest (first words 1.15 s, drafts 1.51 s) but changed one time format: "7pm" became "7 pm". The spec asks for times exactly as written.
- GPT-6-Luna is almost as fast (first words 1.25 s, drafts 1.60 s) and kept every fact. Its one flag is wording, not a fact: "Thursday 2pm" became "Thursday at 2pm", which every model and Claude also did.
- GPT-6.1-Sol kept every fact but is about twice as slow (drafts 3.34 s).

**Warm mode: go.** A waiting process brings first words from about 2.5 s (cold) to about 1.2 s.

**Against Claude.** With a warm engine, GPT-6-Luna (1.25 s first words, 1.60 s drafts) and Claude Sonnet (1.15 s, 1.78 s on the latest run) are on par. Either is well inside the 4 second target.
